# Incident exchange: driver, witness, insurer

Open the Expo app on one phone and `http://macserver:3100/intact/insurer` on the computer. For an independent witness contribution, use a second device or a separate browser profile.

## Quick visual demo

1. Phone → Auto → Community → **I was in an incident**.
2. Open **Try an illustrated example**. Show the scene, two perspectives, and their unknown capture metadata.
3. On the computer, choose the same `PX-DEMO` reference in the insurer queue.
4. Expand a file's integrity section. Say: "Pixie hashes the uploaded file. That proves which bytes the reviewer received; it does not prove who recorded it or what happened."
5. Enter a review note and accept a perspective for case review. The phone reflects the decision within eight seconds.
6. Export the ZIP. It contains the files, their hashes, reported facts, and review history.
7. On the phone, choose **Use this in my recovery plan**. Show the shared incident reference in the existing Community flow.

## Complete live loop

1. Driver reports the incident and shares a test photo or clip. Use the Queen & Spadina example to avoid geocoding delays.
2. Driver opens a witness request with $2 demo credit.
3. On the second device, switch to Bystander, open that request, and contribute another test file with permission.
4. Insurer opens both perspectives. Point to missing facts and the separation between the reporter's statement and the uploaded material.
5. Insurer accepts the witness file with a note. Return to the witness Home tab to show the $2 simulated balance: $1 Home and $1 Auto. Open Compare, select Auto, and show the full $2 toward one simulated Auto payment. Switch to Home to show its allocation is now $0. This is one shared balance.
6. Close the case with a note. New uploads stop, the request pauses, and the history remains available.

Say: "The same incident record follows the customer into recovery and the reviewer into an evidence workspace. The phone asks for consent, the API keeps the actual files and their hashes, and the insurer keeps responsibility for the decision."

Call the savings a simulated one-time insurance credit. No insurer has approved a real discount, and the quoted premium stays unchanged. Credit requires an accepted witness contribution, not an insurance claim. Do not call the illustrations camera footage, claim that the prototype verifies a device, or describe a hash as proof of authenticity. There is no background push broadcast and no real claim submission.
